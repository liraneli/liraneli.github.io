Crete

~1h50 from TLV to Heraklion (HER); Chania (CHQ) also served in summer. A rental car is the practical choice (airport pickup, ~€40-60/day in June). The Heraklion–Hersonissos–Malia coast road has a frequent KTEL bus (every 20-30 min, ~€3), so hotels on that strip work without a car. Organized half/full-day excursions sell at every hotel desk.

Grown-ups: history and landscape

  • Knossos Palace – Minoan palace, 5 km south of Heraklion, 25-35 min from Hersonissos
  • Heraklion Archaeological Museum – The Minoan collection, pairs with Knossos
  • Spinalonga island – Venetian fortress and leper colony island, boat from Elounda/Plaka, 45 min east of Hersonissos
  • Lassithi Plateau and Dikteon Cave – Mountain plateau with windmills and the cave of Zeus, 1 h from Hersonissos
  • Rethymno old town and Fortezza – Venetian harbour and fortress, 1 h west of Heraklion
  • Chania Venetian harbour – Best old town on the island, 2 h from Heraklion
  • Samaria Gorge – 16 km gorge hike, full day, from the Chania side
  • Elafonisi and Balos beaches – Pink-sand lagoon and the Balos lagoon, far west, full-day trips
  • Agios Nikolaos – Lake-town, 30 min east of Hersonissos

Kids: out of the hotel

  • Cretaquarium (Gournes) – Large Mediterranean aquarium, 15 min from Hersonissos
  • Dinosauria Park (Gournes) – Animatronic dinosaur park next to the aquarium
  • Acqua Plus water park (Hersonissos) – Big slide park in the hills above Hersonissos
  • Watercity (Anopolis) – Water park between Heraklion and Hersonissos
  • Labyrinth Park (Hersonissos) – Minotaur-themed maze and activity park
  • Aquaworld (Hersonissos) – Small reptile and marine rescue aquarium

Rhodes

~1h40 from TLV to Rhodes (RHO). Compact island: Rhodes Town, Faliraki, Kallithea and Ixia are 10-25 min apart. KTEL/RODA buses run the east coast (Rhodes Town–Faliraki–Kolymbia–Lindos) every 30-60 min, ~€2.5-5.5; taxis are plentiful and fixed-price. A car is useful for Lindos, Kamiros and the west coast but not essential from Faliraki or Ixia.

Grown-ups: history and landscape

  • Rhodes Old Town – UNESCO medieval walled city: Palace of the Grand Master, Street of the Knights, Archaeological Museum
  • Lindos Acropolis and village – Hilltop acropolis above a whitewashed village, 45 min south of Faliraki
  • Kallithea Springs – Restored Italian-era thermal baths and cove between Rhodes Town and Faliraki
  • Ancient Kamiros – Hellenistic city ruins on the west coast
  • Filerimos monastery and Ialyssos acropolis – Hill above Ixia with views over both coasts
  • Monolithos castle and Prasonisi – Knights castle on a crag and the kite-surf sandbar at the southern tip, full-day drive
  • Tsambika monastery and beach – Viewpoint monastery above a long sandy beach

Kids: out of the hotel

  • Faliraki Water Park – Largest water park in Greece, in Faliraki
  • Rhodes Aquarium (Hydrobiological Station) – Small 1930s aquarium at the tip of Rhodes Town
  • Valley of the Butterflies – Shaded valley with Jersey tiger moths, June-September is the season
  • Anthony Quinn Bay and Ladiko – Sheltered swimming coves next to Faliraki
  • Seven Springs – Short walk through a water tunnel to a lake
